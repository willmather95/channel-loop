github.com/willmather95/channel-loop

A channel loop that learns which changes pay.

It reads the warehouse, makes one change a day through hard guardrails, measures it against campaigns it did not touch, and updates what it believes.
ads-guardrail-agent capslanding-page-lab stats diff-in-diff holdoutidempotent daily tick hash-chained journal
  1. Observe 14 days of DuckDB warehouse data
  2. Hypothesize one change per kind, with a $ estimate
  3. Choose by Bayesian belief in each kind of estimate
  4. Act only through the guardrails and the kill switch
  5. Measure 9 days later vs untouched campaigns
  6. Update beliefs; replay any decision from the log
